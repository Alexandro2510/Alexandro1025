I am a Master's student in Mathematics at the **Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas (CIMAT)** in Guanajuato, Mexico, advised by Luis Núñez Betancourt and Federico Castillo. Previously, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the **Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León (UANL)**.

I am currently applying to **Ph.D. programs in Mathematics** starting in **Fall 2027**.

## Research Interests
My research focuses on **Commutative Algebra**, particularly in prime characteristic. My current work involves:
* **Frobenius methods** and rings with **Finite F-Representation Type (FFRT)**, including quotients of polynomial rings by monomial ideals and affine semigroup rings.
* **Local cohomology modules** and $R\{F\}$-modules.
* Computational commutative algebra using **Macaulay2**.

For my Master's thesis, I am studying the support of local cohomology modules and establishing FFRT properties for specific classes of rings in positive characteristic.

## Teaching & Outreach
I have over ten years of experience teaching mathematics at both the university and high school levels. Prior to joining CIMAT, I served as a lecturer at **UANL** and the **Universidad del Valle de México (UVM)**, teaching courses in Group Theory, Ring and Field Theory, Commutative Algebra, Mathematical Analysis, Linear Algebra, and Calculus.

Alongside my research and teaching, I run the educational YouTube channel [**La leyenda de Noether**](https://www.youtube.com/@LaleyendadeNoether), where I share videos on commutative algebra, problem-solving walkthroughs, and mathematical outreach in Spanish.
