My research lies in **Commutative Algebra**, with a primary focus on prime characteristic methods, **Finite F-Representation Type (FFRT)**, and **local cohomology**. I am also interested in computational methods in commutative algebra using **Macaulay2**.

---

## Master's Thesis & Poster (CIMAT)
**Institution:** Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas (CIMAT), Guanajuato, Mexico  
**Advisors:** Luis Núñez Betancourt and Federico Castillo

My Master's thesis investigates structural properties of rings in positive characteristic $p > 0$ via the Frobenius endomorphism, alongside the support of local cohomology modules:

* **Finite F-Representation Type (FFRT):** I proved that quotient rings of polynomial rings by monomial ideals $S/I$ over $F$-finite fields have Finite F-Representation Type. Currently, I am extending this analysis to numerical and affine semigroup rings $K[A]$, studying the decomposition of Frobenius pushforwards $F_*^e(R)$ and explicit generator sets for Frobenius summands (including bucket modules via Shibuta's approach).
* **Local Cohomology:** I study the support of local cohomology modules $H_I^i(M)$ and their structure as $R\{F\}$-modules in prime characteristic.

## Computational Algebra (Macaulay2)
Alongside theoretical proofs, I develop **Macaulay2** routines to test conjectures and compute invariants in positive characteristic, including:
* Algorithms to identify direct summands of $F_*^e(R)$ and verify isomorphism classes of $R$-modules for affine semigroup rings.
* Computations of Betti tables, $\operatorname{Ext}$ modules, associated primes, and Cohen–Macaulay properties for monomial and toric rings.
